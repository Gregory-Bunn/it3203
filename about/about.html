About the Project

This project aims to provide a brief yet informative overview of web browsers, their history, architecture, and how their role evolves in the near future. It explores how over the past three decades web browsers have evolved from simple text-based browsers meant for researchers to advanced tools capable of multimedia support, used by ordinary people all around the world every day.
